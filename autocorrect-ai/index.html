❓ FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What makes AutoCorrect AI different from Grammarly?
+
AutoCorrect AI goes beyond grammar and spelling. It includes AI text detection, AI humanization, tone transformation, readability scoring, content generation (blog posts, LinkedIn, emails), voice typing, text-to-speech, OCR, PDF editing, and a full AI chat panel — all in one platform. We also explain every correction so you learn while you write.
Does AutoCorrect AI work in real-time?
+
Yes! Our AI analyzes your text continuously as you type. Corrections, suggestions, readability scores, and tone detection all update within milliseconds of your last keystroke. There's no need to click a "check" button.
Which languages does AutoCorrect AI support?
+
AutoCorrect AI supports grammar checking in English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Chinese, Arabic, Hindi, and many more. The translator supports 100+ languages. Voice typing works in all languages supported by your browser's speech recognition.
Is my writing data private and secure?
+
Absolutely. Your documents are stored locally in your browser (localStorage) in the demo version. The production version uses end-to-end encryption, HTTPS, JWT authentication, and is GDPR compliant. We never sell your data to third parties. You can delete your data at any time.
Can I use AutoCorrect AI for academic writing?
+
Yes! AutoCorrect AI has a dedicated Academic Writing mode that helps with essay structure, research writing, citation formatting (APA, MLA, Chicago, Harvard), and maintains appropriate academic tone. The Flesch-Kincaid readability score helps you calibrate complexity for your target audience.
How accurate is the AI detection feature?
+
Our AI detection heuristic analyzes multiple signals including sentence length variance, transition word density, first-person usage patterns, and common AI phrases. It provides a probability score with a confidence level. Note that no AI detector is 100% accurate — treat results as one signal among many, not as definitive proof.
Do you offer a free trial for Pro?
+
Yes! We offer a 14-day free trial of the Pro plan with no credit card required. You get full access to all Pro features including unlimited words, AI rewriting, AI detection, plagiarism checking, and advanced analytics. After the trial, you can choose to upgrade or continue on the Free plan.
Does AutoCorrect AI work offline?
+
The basic spell checker and grammar rules work offline. AI features like rewriting and content generation require an internet connection as they communicate with our AI servers. We're developing a PWA (Progressive Web App) version that will support full offline editing with sync when reconnected.
